### Native corner seam correction (2026-09-21)

The exported L-corner's outer planes are X=-1 and Z=2, while its inner planes are X=-0.8 and Z=1.8. Straight panels are normalized by their rear face. The assembly previously placed the corner outer plane at the straight panel rear plane, and reserved the full two-module return without accounting for that translation. This left a visible strip of daylight at each junction.

The resolver now aligns the corner inner plane with the panel rear plane and derives the panel interval from the transformed corner endpoint. Solid fillers retain floor-scaled thickness rather than a separate upper cap. Window proportions and the native meshes remain unchanged; no extra covering strips or draw calls are introduced.

Bounding-box rear planes were not compatible mounting surfaces: native windows, wall sheets and door frames have different depths; some are open sheets, and some WhiteBrick centre sections require matching neighbours. The previous mathematical corner test did not exercise these actual mesh connections.

- The asset workflow now measures structural perimeter planes, intended packing cells, frame apertures and stair dimensions into cityNativeModules.ts. A tiny decorative overhang no longer determines the width of an entire wall bay.
- All native wall faces align to the building's exterior boundary. Adaptive L returns shorten around narrow wings and full entrance modules; plain native tiles fill remaining wall intervals. Complete WhiteBrick windows replace centre-only sections in standalone bays.
- Shared panel geometry gains inward perimeter returns where source sheets are open. Window and door apertures remain open for their native inserts. Vertex colours, UVs and normals are preserved; returns are merged into the existing material geometry, without another draw per instance.

Openings and surrounding wall infill follow the assembly. Native stair height defines the service-door landing; the previous reduced service-door scale is removed.
- Foundations and supporting slabs use square mating edges. Raised fascia courses extend to their common outer corner so decorative borders connect too.
- Shapes remain bounded by the plot. No full solid backing cube or interior-room system is introduced.

### Readable native window glass (2026-09-21)

The window primitives had valid outward normals and preserved colour attributes. The shared City glass shader intentionally crushed the diffuse colour toward black and mixed only 20% reflection with a very dark ground hemisphere. An elevated map camera reflects that ground hemisphere, leaving window panels almost black despite correctly textured surrounding masonry.

Glass now retains a blue-grey body tint, uses a daylight ground-to-horizon reflection gradient and a stronger bounded Fresnel contribution. This applies to native panes and procedural glass, preserving instancing, opaque depth writes and the existing sun glint. It introduces no textures, transparency sorting, environment-map allocation or draw calls. The glass shader cache revision is bumped. Four-family browser screenshots verify white-brick, brick, marble and metal at the city viewing angle. No schema, provider or worker change is needed.

### Single native entrance and consistent masonry colour (2026-09-21)

The Monday Studio 8 fixture (demo index 63) used accents, a brick native frame and the shop archetype. Accents previously kept its procedural glass entrance and decorative shop jambs while also adding an independently scaled native frame and leaf. Both native finish modes now use the same measured frame/aperture fit. Their procedural entrance and shop jambs remain loading fallbacks, suppressed only once the frame and leaf are available. Accents mount the complete assembly slightly forward of their procedural wall; full facades retain the cut opening.

Inspection of actual Brick_Plain_3 vertices found near-zero RGB at the bottom and white at the top, whereas neighbouring window masonry was white throughout. Multiplying the authored albedo by that module-local ramp caused the discontinuous black shading. Textured native materials now ignore vertex tint while retaining albedo, normal and roughness maps. Geometry attributes are preserved and untextured vertex-coloured props retain their colour behavior. This adds no textures or draw calls.
