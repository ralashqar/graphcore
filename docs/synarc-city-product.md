# Synarc City

Money buys geography. Engagement creates life.

## Launch contract

Public, map-like 3D discovery at `/city`. No avatar or movement keys. Cumulative GBP placement purchases determine a movable location and a six-tier skyline. Payments buy sponsored placement, not fixed land, resale rights, investment returns or guaranteed traffic. Eligible Land Value excludes taxes, refunds and disputed funds. Equal value retains the earlier incumbent.

Begin with 400 saleable plots on a 20 by 20 arrangement around a separate plaza and crossed avenues. Expansion appends outer rings without changing existing priorities. Suspended businesses are removed from public allocation; their financial history remains intact.

## Journeys

- Visitors explore without authentication, search, select buildings and follow business links. Accounts unlock saves and recorded offer claims. Claims mean code reveals or offer-link intent, not verified purchases.
- Business owners import metadata or enter branding manually, upload images/video, preview a constrained property, verify website control, submit for review, and purchase after approval. Published content stays live while edits await approval.
- Administrators review exact submitted revisions, reject with a reason, suspend unsafe businesses and inspect payment exceptions.

One person may be a consumer and business owner. Launch exposes one owner and one business per account. Teams and multiple properties are deferred.

## Visual direction

Refined textured downtown using the Quaternius MegaKit: brick and metal architecture, forest-green controls, off-white surfaces, branded signs, bounded tower heights and a landscaped pedestrian plaza. Four 24 m plots share each street block; buildings retain at least 4 m setback. Desktop uses a right-side property drawer; mobile uses a bottom panel. The HTML directory is accessible and survives WebGL failure. Reduced motion snaps camera and rank movements.

## MVP and later

MVP: map, indexed search, business onboarding, domain verification, manual moderation, GBP Checkout, atomic allocation, one offer, saves, claim history, basic analytics, rank events, downloadable share cards. £10 minimum / £50,000 per checkout. Tier thresholds: £10, £100, £500, £2,000, £10,000, £50,000.

Later: themed competitive districts, temporary auctions, billboard rental, teams, campaign schedules, funded rewards, bespoke architecture, sandboxed experiences. No arbitrary HTML/JS, NFTs, avatars, interiors or unverified conversion claims.

## Pilot acceptance

Validate onboarding completion, checkout conversion, repeat placement purchases, returning visitors, qualified property views, saves, claims and outbound clicks. Test 400 and 2,000 occupied plots; target 30 FPS mobile / 60 FPS desktop. Demonstration businesses must be labelled and never mixed with live inventory.
